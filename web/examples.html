Data sources and the results

This page shows what each data source sends in, and what the system produces.

These examples are drawn and generated. They are not real footage or real vehicles.

The problem

More cameras and sensors watch more places than people can review.

Sending all video to a central server is slow and costly.

Watching everyone constantly puts privacy at risk.

Nobody can easily prove records were not changed.

What this system shows

Spot unusual readings and rank alerts.

Send short summaries from the camera.

Pick the fastest response route.

Protect privacy in a record.

Keep a tamper-proof log.

A simulation on made-up data. It shows how such a system could be designed, not how a real city runs.
